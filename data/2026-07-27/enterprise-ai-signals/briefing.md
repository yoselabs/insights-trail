## Cross-Source Patterns

**Pattern 1: [new] The FDE Race Is the New Enterprise AI Deployment Infrastructure**

Four programs totaling ~$14.5B in committed capital (OpenAI Deployment Co $10B, Microsoft Frontier $2.5B, AWS FDE $1B, Anthropic JV $1.5B) all share the same architecture: engineers embedded at client sites, outcome-based measurement, client self-sufficiency as exit condition. The convergence of model vendors, hyperscalers, and cloud providers on this model in a 6-week window (May 11–July 2, 2026) is not coincidence — it signals that enterprise AI adoption is failing at the deployment/integration layer, not at the model layer. Traditional SIs (McKinsey, Capgemini, Bain & Company are OpenAI Deployment Co investors) are being structurally disrupted.

**Pattern 2: [new] Cloud Hyperscalers Posting AI-Driven Revenue Records While Enterprises Report ROI Shortfalls**

Google Cloud +82% YoY ($24.8B), Intel DCAI +59% ($6.3B), AWS Q1 +28% ($37.6B) — the infrastructure layer is growing at rates not seen since early cloud era. But Domino (57% ROI shortfall, two-year plateau), Kyndryl (only 11% achieve both top AI goals), DoiT (79% overspend), and PwC (56% no benefit) all confirm that enterprise buyers are not converting infrastructure investment into measurable returns. The disconnect is sharpening: hyperscaler AI cloud revenue is accelerating while enterprise ROI surveys are not improving.

**Pattern 3: [update] Restructuring Is No Longer Just Cuts — It's Org Design for AI Collaboration**

Monday.com (20%, reduced management layers for AI agents), Cloudflare (20%, eliminated "measurers"), Oracle (13%, SEC blames AI deployment), PayPal (20%, $1.5B savings target). Three companies cut 20% of their workforces in the same quarter citing AI not as "cost savings" but as organizational redesign. Cloudflare's "measurers" framing and Monday.com's "built around AI agents" framing represent a more specific claim than prior AI layoff rationales — they name which functional roles AI has made structurally redundant (middle management measurement functions) and which it requires more of (implementation support, on-site customer engagement, engineering). This is the first cohort of layoffs with an explicit functional taxonomy.

**Pattern 4: [ongoing] The Infrastructure Commitment vs. Enterprise ROI Divergence Continues to Widen**

Aligned $40B acquisition + Fireworks $1.5B + IREN $2.8B contracts + Oracle $55.7B capex + Google Cloud $514B backlog. Infrastructure capital is committing for 3-5+ years. Enterprise ROI surveys (Domino 57%, Kyndryl 11%, DoiT 15% can prove ROI) are not closing the gap. The infrastructure layer is building for the 6-9% of enterprises that are actual high performers; the majority remain in a cycle of AI deployment without measurable returns.
