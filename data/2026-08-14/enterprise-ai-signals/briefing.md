## Cross-Source Patterns

### Pattern 1: AI Infrastructure Revenue Is Compounding at Hyperscaler Rates — Across Multiple Layers

Cisco (AI orders $9.3B FY2026, +4.5×) and CoreWeave ($2.6B Q2, +112%; $104B backlog) both report that AI infrastructure demand is outpacing their ability to fulfill it. IREN ($2.8B customer contracts, 45% prepaid), Axe Compute ($1.3B contracts), and CoreWeave ($104B+ locked backlog) all show enterprises locking in multi-year compute capacity commitments. The supply-constraint signal: CoreWeave's $25B in new Q3 commitments was signed after the quarter closed, and backlog grew 246% YoY on $12-13B annual revenue — a 10× backlog-to-revenue ratio.

**Platforms:** Web 🌐 — Cisco investor release, CoreWeave investor release, SkillSyncer, IREN press releases

### Pattern 2: AI-Driven Acquisitions Are Targeting Operational Context, Not Models

Autodesk/$3.6B MaintainX and Schneider/$350M AiDASH share a common logic: the acquirers are not buying AI models but AI-enriched **operational context data** (maintenance records, satellite vegetation risk, asset performance under real conditions). Both acquirers explicitly state that AI is only as accurate as the contextual data beneath it. The same logic drove Okta/$200M Permiso — acquiring not a model but behavioral intelligence about how AI agents act in production. Enterprise AI M&A in 2026 is converging on the data and context layer, not the model layer.

**Platforms:** Web 🌐 — Autodesk news, ESG Today/AiDASH, Okta PR, TechCrunch

### Pattern 3: Physical / Industrial AI Is a Confirmed Funding Category

Prometheus ($12B at $41B, Jun 11) and Atoms ($1.7B, Jul 22) are both raising at premium valuations for the same thesis: AI applied to physical systems (industrial, materials, manufacturing) rather than software workflows. Both are backed by institutional capital (Goldman, JPMorgan, BofA as debt partners, a16z as lead). Etched ($300M at $10B) adds inference chip infrastructure for the same market. This is a distinct category from enterprise software AI — it is AI applied to physical production processes.

**Platforms:** Web 🌐 — TechCrunch/Atoms, Crunchbase weekly, CNBC/Prometheus (prior)

### Pattern 4: Governance Gap Remains the Single Largest Blocker — Now Quantified by Gartner

Gartner (N=3,400) predicts 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027 — the cancellations are governance and ROI failures, not technology failures. This aligns with: McKinsey N=10,000 (81% no bottom-line gains); Schellman N=525 (74% believe audit-ready, 27% are); DoiT N=500 (79% overspend, 15% prove ROI); Plug and Play (50% production companies can't measure results).
