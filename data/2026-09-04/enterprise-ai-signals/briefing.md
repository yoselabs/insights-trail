## Cross-Source Patterns

### Pattern 1: Enterprise Demand for AI Infrastructure Is Broadening Beyond Hyperscalers
**Sources:** Dell Q2 (6,500+ customers; sovereign + enterprise expanding); Crusoe $13B Jane Street deal (quant trading); Gimlet Labs $300M (chip-agnostic routing for heterogeneous enterprise); Army TITAN $192M (defense production)
**Signal:** The demand base for AI infrastructure contracts is now multi-sector (finance, defense, enterprise IT) — no longer just hyperscaler-concentrated. Dell's 6,500+ customer base and Jane Street's $13B compute commitment confirm financial sector is building own AI infrastructure at scale.

### Pattern 2: Agent Governance Is Transitioning From Startup to Platform Feature
**Sources:** Broadcom AgentMinder GA (Sep 2, incumbent infra vendor, 36M daily API calls); JetStream Clearance (per-action blocking); Okta Agent SSO; Cloudflare WriteGuard; AccuKnox AgentZ; Tricentis AgentScore; AIR Security $50M
**Signal:** Six dedicated agent governance products launched or GA in the past week; first major incumbent infrastructure vendor (Broadcom/VMware) ships production-grade agent governance. Category is exiting startup phase. New pattern: pre-execution blocking (JetStream) vs post-log review — governance hardening from audit to control.

### Pattern 3: Anthropic Is Simultaneously Cutting Prices and Restricting Access — Ahead of IPO
**Sources:** Fable 5.1 cache -75%; Sonnet 5 $2/$10/M permanent; EFS customer-controlled data; Mythos 5.1 gated (vetted orgs only); IPO prospectus post-Labor Day
**Signal:** Anthropic's Sep 1 triple launch combines price aggression (capture volume at scale) with access restriction (Mythos for vetted orgs) and governance lock-in (EFS switching costs). The timing — one week before the public S-1 — positions the company with concrete enterprise metrics: high-frequency deployment + regulated-industry compliance.

### Pattern 4: ROI Gap Is Structural and Stable — Production Up, Earnings Flat
**Sources:** Domino N=639 (57% ROI flat vs 2025); McKinsey N=1,719 (37% EBIT impact flat); Kyndryl N=1,100 (57% AI in core processes, 23% workforce-ready); Plug and Play (50% can't measure ROI); KPMG N=2,000+ (7% established ROI)
**Signal:** Every major survey released in the trailing 6 weeks confirms the same pattern: production deployment is expanding rapidly, but financial impact is not growing proportionally. The Domino finding that the 57% ROI failure rate is unchanged from 2025 is particularly significant — it suggests the ROI gap is not closing even as production capability improves by 5–10pp per year.
