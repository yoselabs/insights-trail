- Platforms: Anthropic.com · Dark Reading · AndroidHeadlines · AI Weekly

**3. Wharton N=1,416 + prior surveys converge: governance architecture (CAIO + single platform) predicts ROI more than AI product choice**
- Wharton-GBK (Oct 13, N=1,416): CAIO → 58% vs 24% strong ROI; single platform → 50% vs 9% strong ROI

- Pattern: The data is now directionally consistent — structural governance factors (CAIO, platform consolidation, growth-not-efficiency metrics) separate the 7–28% achieving strong ROI from the rest
- Platforms: Wharton · Forbes · KPMG · McKinsey · PwC

**4. Enterprise AI platform integrations deepening via MCP — Workday, Anthropic, Sana all use MCP as connective tissue**

- Google Gemini Agent (Oct 8): cross-model (including Claude) via open standards

- Platforms: Workday newsroom · Google Cloud · DocuSign
