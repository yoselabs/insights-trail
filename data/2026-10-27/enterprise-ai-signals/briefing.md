### 8. [new] Faros AI 2026 (22K Devs, 4K Teams): The Productivity Paradox at Scale

**Claim:** Faros AI's 2026 enterprise telemetry study (22K developers, 4K teams) found AI coding tools create apparent throughput gains masking quality and review degradation at scale — more code, more bugs, more incidents.

**Evidence:**
- **Sample:** 22,000 developers across 4,000 enterprise teams (updated from earlier 10K/1,255 dataset)
- **Throughput:** +21% tasks completed, +98% PRs merged (team-level)
- **Quality degradation:** +51% PR size; +28% bugs per PR; 5× median review time; 3× incidents per PR; 10× code churn
- **Delivery:** Overall delivery velocity flat or declining at organizational level
- **Root cause:** Code writing = only 10–21% of developer time; AI accelerates that slice while review/design bottlenecks compound
- **HN amplification:** "AI coding agents generate more code, but not more software" (365 pts, 314 comments) — https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50059523
